# Field Companion guide: questions and answers

## Do I need to install an app?

No. You use WhatsApp for the chat and a browser link on your phone for the walk. Allow the camera and the microphone when the browser asks.

## How long does a walkthrough link work?

72 hours. It works on the first phone that opens it. If it has expired, or you opened it on the wrong phone, ask for a new link in the chat.

## What can I send in the chat?

Text, voice notes, photos (JPEG, PNG or WebP), PDF and Word files, locations and contact cards, up to 15 MB each.

## Is the walk recorded?

Audio is never recorded or stored. Your speech is turned into text, and that processing happens in the EU.

## Is it a person?

No. It is an AI assistant, and it says so in its first message. It can be wrong, so check what matters before you rely on it.

## Where is my report?

It arrives as a PDF in the chat after you submit, usually within a minute. If more than 24 hours have passed since your last message, WhatsApp only lets us send a short "report ready" message. Reply to it and the PDF follows.

## What if I have no signal on site?

Photos and changes wait on the phone and are sent when you are back online.

## Can I see my cases on the web?

Yes. Send "show me my cases" and you get a sign-in link that lasts 15 minutes.

## Can I use another language?

Field Companion speaks English, Dutch and Spanish. Send "help", then choose Change language.

## How do I stop the tips?

Send "no tips". Send "tips on" to turn them back on. Each tip is shown at most once.

## How long is my data kept?

As long as your organisation has chosen. Practice visits are the exception: they are kept for 7 days, and they are not counted in reports or figures.
